Protocol

Fixed before modelling: 5 outer folds over participants (10 test / 40 train each), 0.5–3.5 s cue-locked window, CSP+LDA with inner grid on training participants only. Full text: docs/evaluation_protocol.md.

Limits

  • Engineering results for one CSP+LDA pipeline on 50 participants of one public subset. Not clinical, assistive, or neuroscience conclusions.
  • Group mean above chance does not mean every person decodes well. Median participant accuracy is about 58%; many individuals cannot be separated from guessing at this trial count.
  • Test trials were scored without extra cleaning; training-only rejection removed 10–29 trials per fold.
  • Only five outer folds — fold SD is a rough spread measure, not a precise uncertainty on the mean.
  • Neural comparison is EEGNet-R only; do not generalise to EEGNet or deep models in general.
